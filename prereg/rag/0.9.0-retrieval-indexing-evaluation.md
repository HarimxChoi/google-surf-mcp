# Retrieval and indexing evaluation

Date: 2026-08-25

## Hypotheses

1. Multilingual dense reranking improves held-out `nDCG@10` by at least 0.03 without reducing exact-code Recall@10 by more than 0.01.
2. Reusing Tree-sitter workers reduces full-code parsing time by at least 20% with identical symbol and relation digests and peak RSS below 1.25 times baseline.
3. Building the source full-text index after the initial bulk insert reduces ready-to-search time by at least 25% with identical top-10 results.

## Measurements

- Recall@5, Recall@10, nDCG@10 and MRR@10 by lexical, semantic, code and multi-hop strata
- Cold and warm p50 and p95 latency
- Peak RSS and model-load latency
- Inventory, source write, code parse, graph build and total indexing time
- Symbol, relation and retrieval result digests

## Gates

- Cross-project leakage is zero.
- Valid provenance is at least 0.99.
- Dense warm p95 is at most 500 ms.
- Dense peak RSS increase is at most 512 MiB.
- Indexing challengers preserve all result digests.

## Falsifiers

- Dense `nDCG@10` gain is below 0.03 in any protected semantic or cross-language stratum.
- Dense loses more than 0.01 Recall@10 on exact-code cases or exceeds either resource gate.
- Persistent workers save less than 20%, change any output digest or exceed 1.25 times baseline RSS.
- Post-load full-text construction saves less than 25%, changes top-10 results or exposes an active snapshot before the index is ready.

## Deferred

- Cross-encoder and late-interaction rerankers
- Human-reviewed citation and assertion-span labels
- ANN indexes
- Obsidian export
